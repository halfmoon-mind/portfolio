# May Be Clean

May Be Clean is a mobile application that provides, on a map-based interface, places where you can practice eco-friendly consumption.
It makes it easy to find places for sustainable consumption such as zero-waste shops, eco-friendly product stores, refill stations, and vegan restaurants.

It was developed using Flutter, and by integrating the Google Maps API, users can explore nearby eco-friendly places based on their current location.
We implemented features such as real-time data updates, user authentication, and place reviews and ratings.

Through a community feature that lets users register and review eco-friendly places themselves, we are continuously expanding the database,
and by providing information and tips on eco-friendly consumption, we are contributing to raising users' environmental awareness.

## Features

- Map-based exploration of eco-friendly places
- Filtering by category and distance
- User review and rating system
- User-driven registration of eco-friendly shops
- Eco-friendly consumption guides and information

## Tech Stack

- **Flutter**: Cross-platform mobile app development
- **Google Maps API**: Map service integration
- **GetX**: State management and routing
- **Spring Boot**: Backend server
